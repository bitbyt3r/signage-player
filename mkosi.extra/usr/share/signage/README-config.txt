SIGNAGE PLAYER INSTALL STICK

1. Edit config.toml on this drive (WiFi, display, dashboard).
2. Plug the stick into the player and boot from USB.
3. The player installs itself to its internal disk, WIPING IT, after a
   countdown on screen. Unplug the stick or power off during the countdown
   to cancel.
4. When it says so, remove the stick. It reboots into the installed system.
